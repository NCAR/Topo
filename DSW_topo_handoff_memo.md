**Conventions used here:** Fortran names in `code`; "cube" = the 3000×3000×6 equiangular cubed-sphere intermediate grid; "target" = the CAM grid (ne30pg3 unless stated); "ridge", "ridgelet" and "tile ridge" all mean the per-column subgrid ridge objects.

## 1. Scientific goal in one paragraph

CAM's orographic gravity-wave scheme (`gw_rdg_calc`, in `gw_rdg.F90`) carries up to `prdg` ridges per column, each with height `mxdis`, width `hwdth`, crest length `clngt`, orientation `angll`/`anglx`, anisotropy `anixy`, and other descriptors, from which it computes mountain-wave stress, wave breaking, low-level blocking, and — through the `taudsw` branch — downslope-windstorm (DSW) enhancement. The research goal is to use this machinery as a **cheap downscaling tool for surface wind, temperature and humidity in DSW fire-weather events** (Santa Ana, Gatlinburg 2016, Italian bora/foehn cases), driven by ~100 km reanalysis, with no high-resolution simulation. Two codebases serve this: the **NCAR Topo** software (generates the ridge descriptors) and a private **offline driver (GW_UnitTest)** that runs CAM's GW physics on reanalysis columns without a CAM build. A JFSP (BLM Joint Fire Science Program, task 27-2-1) proposal using this material was submitted in September 2026.

## 3. Topo pipeline — how ridges are made

`create_topo.py` (CLI → YAML → defaults) builds a case directory and runs `cube_to_target.x`. Case names encode options: `{ogrid}_Sco{scale}{_Sfi##}{_Rw###}_{tag}`, e.g. `ne30pg3_Sco100_Rw050_v203_xtra_e02`.

Inside `cube_to_target.F90` → `ridge_ana.F90`, the chain is:

1. **Smoothing** (`smooth_topo_cube.F90`): Laplacian smoother on the cube gives the resolved topography; `terr_dev` = raw − smoothed is what the ridge finder sees. A **band-pass option** was added: `--fine_smoothing_scale` (CLI, tag `_Sfi##`) re-applies the Laplacian to `terr_dev` with `nu_lap_fine = 20.0E7*(fine/100)**2` and `smooth_phis_numcycle_fine`; skipped gracefully when unset. Note: the iteration count does *not* change the smoothing (dt = 16/numcycle), only `nu_lap` does.
2. **Peak finding** (`find_local_maxes`): local maxima of `terr_dev` over all 6 panels. (A segfault when restricting to one panel came from `npeaks` being counted over 6 panels but filled for one, leaving `peaks%ip = -99`; count and fill must share bounds.)
3. **Per-peak window analysis**: a `(2*PSW+1)²` window (`PSW=32`) is cut around each peak (`suba`), rotated by candidate angles (`rotbyx`, `rotby4` nearest-neighbour), and a crest profile is fitted → `mxdis`, `hwdth`, `clngth`, `anglx`, `riseq`, `fallq`, `mxvrx/mxvry` (→ `anixy = mxvrx/(mxvrx+mxvry+1e-4)`). `--ridge_window_ratio` (CLI `-w`, tag `_Rw###`, default 1.0) scales the analysis window; NINT-consistent tagging (`floor(x+0.5)`).
4. **Painting ridges onto the cube** (`paintridge2cube`, `color_on_profi`): each ridge paints a 1-pixel crest (`uniqidC`) and a wedge (`uniqwgC`, with `wedgoC` the signed cross-crest coordinate). Conflicts resolved by `subblk >= QC` max-selection in a rotated rectangle of half-width `nhw = MIN(INT(hwdth/4), nsw/2)`. **This reconciliation is where most ridges lose their crest** — of 286,956 ridges only 276,205 crest cells survive globally; many ridges paint nothing. Culling here is the first knob to turn if coverage is too sparse (candidate: the `/4` divisor in `nhw`).
   - Crest-painting extent is controlled by two module parameters `paint_reach_crest`, `paint_reach_wedge` (fraction of `nsw`; `NSWx_lim = MAX(1,MIN(nsw,INT(paint_reach*NSWx+1e-9)))`). **0.5 reproduces the pre-September truncated behaviour; 1.0 (committed) paints the full ridge.** Tested both: ~14 vs 17 min runtime, visually negligible difference in tile fields.
   - Bugs fixed here (Sept): `bxc` uninitialised in `color_on_profi` (garbage ridge IDs); `xloid` integer division; `Y0+ii` instead of `Y0+jj` in four y-bounds guards; `MAX(wedgoMap,0)`; REAL division in centroid averages.
5. **Tiling** (`remapridge2tiles`): for each target column `i` and each painted ridge object `ir`: `MyObject(i,ir)` = ridge id, `LnObject(i,ir)` = number of crest cells of that ridge **inside the column**, `WtObject(i,ir)` = wedge cells inside the column, plus centroids `xloid/yloid` (crest) and `xwoid/ywoid` (wedge) and `lonc_tiles/latc_tiles`. All three accumulators must be zeroed (`MyObject=0; WtObject=0; LnObject=0`) — they were not, and the uninitialised memory produced the −9999 centroid artefacts. `maxtiles` is the max object count over columns (43 for the production ne30pg3 run; 49 in an earlier one) and becomes `nrdg` in the output.
   - `LnObject` conservation was verified exactly: Σ over tiles of `LnObject` equals the cube count of `uniqidC` per ridge, **0 disagreements of 286,956**.
   - In-column crest length: `clnin_tiles(i,ir) = LnObject(i,ir) * grid_length_scale * length_in_square(anglx_tiles(i,ir))`, guarded by `anglx > -900`. `grid_length_scale = R_earth·(π/2)/ncube ≈ 3.336 km`. `length_in_square(anglx)` is the rasterisation correction (1/cos of the angle from the nearest axis) because a diagonal crest of *n* cells is longer than *n* cell widths.
6. **Importance sort** (`lsort_tiles = .TRUE.`, default): tiles in each column are sorted descending on `tkey(k) = mxdis_tiles(i,k) * clnin_tiles(i,k)`; all 24 per-tile arrays are permuted consistently (`permute_row_r8/i4`, local helpers, not intrinsics). Rationale: `effgw ∝ hwdth·clngt/gbxar` and `kwvrdg ∝ 1/hwdth` cancel, blocked drag ∝ `clngt·mxdis`, unblocked ∝ `clngt·mxdis²`; the linear key was chosen for simplicity and because truncation survivors are mostly blocked ridges. The old key `mxdis*clngt` (whole-ridge length) was rejected once CLNGT became in-column.
7. **Output**: `Ridge_tile_map.dat` (Fortran sequential unformatted; readers below) and, new, a **CAM-readable netCDF** `<output>_tiles.nc` written by `wrtncdf_ridge_tiles` (pasted into `cube_to_target.F90`, explicit Makefile object list; guarded by `lridgetiles .and. lfind_ridges .and. .not.ltarget_latlon`). Variables `(ncol,nrdg)` in Fortran = `(nrdg,ncol)` in Python: `MXDIS, HWDTH, ANGLL, ANGLX, ANIXY, CLNGT (= clnin, in-column, metres), CLNGT_RDG (= clngt_tiles*grid_length_scale, whole ridge), WGHTS, RISEQ, FALLQ, LONC, LATC`, fill `EMPTY_TILE=-900`. `ANGLL` comes from a new module `ridge_angles_mod.F90` (`ridge_anglx_to_latlon`, central-difference Jacobian of the gnomonic map, atan2, folded to [0,180)), replacing the legacy `latlonangles` (which had a branch cut and an `acos` precision problem).
   - Per-column Σ CLNGT in the tiles file matches the original (non-tile) topo file: fewer metres per ridge, compensated by more ridges per column.
   - A per-panel `DEBUGOUTPUT` cpp path writes `TerrXY_*.dat` with the raw and rotated windows (`suba_diag`, `rt_diag`) for peaks in a box (`idiag_panel=4, i∈[300,1900], j∈[2300,3000]`, i.e. southern CONUS; 20,560 of 461,489 peaks); each record carries its `ipk`. It is `#undef` by default; flip to `#define` only for figures.

### Geometry facts worth having at hand
- 1-D cube index: `ii = (ip-1)*ncube² + (iy-1)*ncube + ix`.
- Panel 4 holds southern CONUS (San Gabriel ≈ ix 572, iy 2756; Gatlinburg ≈ ix 1717, iy 2696). Panel 1 is centred on Greenwich; **Italy straddles panels 1 and 6**: Tuscany is in the top ~30 rows of panel 1 (β→45° at ~44.2°N for 10.5°E), everything north of ~44.3°N — including the entire Carso/bora region — is on the north-polar panel 6. Plotting code must mask over both.
- Cube +y is exactly north on equatorial panels; +x is ~16.6° off east at San Gabriel.
- `anglx` is measured from +β toward +α; the geographic crest azimuth needs the Jacobian (the projection is not conformal).

## 4. GW_UnitTest — the offline driver

Builds `gw_driver.x` from CAM's `gw_rdg.F90`/`gw_common.F90` plus a thin driver. `create_case.py --type ERA5` sets up a case; runs read per-step files `<root>.YYYY-MM-DD-SSSSS.nc` (hybrid coeffs → `make_pressures`, `geopotential_t`). Frontal and moving-mountain sources are off for this work.

Changes made (all committed):
- **All ridges, not just `nn==1`, are output.** `nc_flexout_mod.F90` gained `ncfile_put_col2d_rdg(varname,field,itime,irdg,nrdg,...)` and `ncfile_put_col3d_rdg`, an `ensure_rdg_dim`, chunking `(ncol,1,1)`/`(ncol,nz,1,1)` with shuffle + deflate 1 (~100× smaller on sparse ridge fields), and `ncfile_set_globals(...,bnd_topo,n_rdg,nrdg_file)` + `copy_topo_provenance` (copies `bnd_topo_GIT_DESCRIBE` etc. from the topo file).
- `dimid_rdg = -1; nrdg_save = 0` reset at the top of `ncfile_init_col` and `ncfile_init` — fixes "NetCDF: Invalid dimension ID" on the second timestep.
- `gw_rdg_calc_mod.F90`: per-ridge writes gated by namelist `lrdg_diag_2d`, `lrdg_diag_3d` (`top_ctl_nl`); `TAUDSW` now writes `taudsw` (it was writing `tauoro`) and `VTRDG` writes `vtrdg` (was `utrdg`); totals `UTRDG/VTRDG/TTRDG` after the ridge loop; dummies declared `(ncol,n_rdg)` not `prdg`.
- `gw_driver.F90`: `n_rdg = min(n_rdg_beta, nrdgs)` from the topo file's ridge dimension, hoisted before the time loop; `ncol` mismatch guard between topo and forcing files.
- Timing on ne30pg3: ~20 s/step compute, ~28 s with full 2-D+3-D ridge output (compression costs ~8 s, not the compute). Tile counts fall ~4× per 5 ridge indices, so `n_rdg_beta ≈ 12–15` captures the physics; 43–49 is affordable for diagnostics.
- Caveat for CAM proper: `prdg = 16` is hardwired in CAM's `gw_drag.F90`; tile files have `nrdg` = 43+, so either cap `n_rdg` or change `prdg` before using tile files in a real CAM run.

### DSW physics as implemented (what decides whether a DSW "fires")
- Stresses: `tauoro = kwvrdg·hdspwv²·ρ·N·U`, `taudsw = kwvrdg·hdspdw²·ρ·N·U`, with `Frx = mxdis·N/U/Fr_c`.
- `do_divstream = .true.` branch: `Fr2 = Fr1·(1 + C_GammaMax·anixy)` for `Frx ≤ Frx0`, ramping to `Fr1` at `Frx1`.
- Namelist in use (`gw_rdg_nl`): `gw_rdg_fr_c = 1.0`, `frx0 = 2`, `frx1 = 3`, `c_betamax_ds = 0.0` (DSW amplification **off**), `c_gammamax = 2`.
- Vertical stress profile is four-layer: `tauoro` above `bwv`; ramp `tauoro→taudsw` between `bwv` and `tlb`; `taudsw + Cd·½ρU²(tlb−z)` below `tlb`. Consequence: `TAU_A_RDG` and `TAU_B_RDG` are identical at the surface by construction — not a bug.
- The SM2000 branch is still viable but unused.

### Surface wind/temperature reconstruction from per-ridge stress (what the figures use)
Inverting `τ ≈ ρ k U N δ²` with `k ≈ 1/hwdth`: `δ = sqrt(hwdth·τ/(ρ N U))`, `u′ = N δ`, `U_tot = UBMSRC + u′`, `u′_DSW = N(δ_dsw − δ_oro)`.
Warming: **`T′ = δ N² T_src / g`** (with `T_src ≈ 280 K`) — *not* `δ·g/c_p`; the descending parcel arrives in an already-warmer environment, so the full dry-adiabatic rate overstates the anomaly.

## 7. What was tried and rejected, and why

- **`acos`-based crest angle conversion (`latlonangles`)** → rejected: branch cut at the dateline and precision loss near 0/180; replaced by Jacobian + atan2.
- **Sort key `mxdis*clngt` (whole-ridge length)** → rejected after CLNGT became in-column; a ridge's drag in a column depends on how much of it is there.
- **`mxdis²` sort key** → considered (unsaturated drag ∝ mxdis²), rejected for simplicity and because truncation survivors are blocked ridges.
- **Crest truncation at `nsw/2`** → lifted (made parametric); 1.0 adopted.
- **`WGHTS` from remap weights for tiles** → not available; `wghts_tiles = area_target·WtObject/idcoun` (geometric fraction) used instead — same units, different provenance.
- **Keeping `DEBUGOUTPUT` on** → rejected (multi-GB files, disk-quota failures with exit status 2).
- **`nn==1`-only ridge output in the driver** → replaced by full `nrdg` output with switches.
- **`δ·g/c_p` warming** → replaced by `δN²T/g`.
- **Right-triangle paint shapes** → replaced by isoceles triangles with the base on the crest.
- **Projecting wind onto the cube-normal image for downwind/upwind** → rejected (shear leak gave ±1 for along-crest wind); true geographic crest normal used.

## 10. Open questions / things nobody has settled

1. **Ridge culling in `paintridge2cube`**: most ridges paint no crest. Is the `hwdth/4` conflict half-width too aggressive? Nobody has swept it.
2. **`prdg=16` vs `nrdg=43`** in production CAM: cap or raise `prdg`? Memory and cost implications unmeasured in CAM itself.
3. **`c_betamax_ds = 0`**: DSW amplification is off in all results so far. The DSW signal seen comes from `taudsw` with `hdspdw` alone. Turning amplification on is an obvious sensitivity experiment.
4. **`wghts_tiles` provenance** (geometric vs remap-weight) — matters if CAM uses WGHTS for anything beyond diagnostics.
5. **`fine_smoothing_scale`** band-pass: implemented and plumbed, never used in a production case.
6. **Sort key**: linear `mxdis·clnin` chosen; `mxdis²` never tested end-to-end.
7. **Humidity/RH** downscaling (promised in the proposal objectives) has no implementation yet; only wind and T′.
8. `xloid/yloid` are not yet on the tiles netCDF (centroids are recomputed in Python from LONC/LATC); adding them was agreed as a convenience.
9. Optional `--paint_reach_*` CLI flags and a Makefile `-DDEBUGOUTPUT` switch were discussed, not done.
10. Weimer et al. 2023 and Gaffin 2009 / Shadbolt et al. 2019 are the key Appalachian DSW references; the proposal bibliography is in `Citations_alphabetized.docx`.
